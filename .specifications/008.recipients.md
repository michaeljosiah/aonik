# 008.recipients

## Objective
Provide recipient CRUD endpoints to replace MTM friends/recipients.

## Scope
- `POST /recipients`
- `PUT /recipients/{recipientId}`
- `GET /recipients/{recipientId}`
- `GET /recipients`
- `DELETE /recipients/{recipientId}`
- `POST /recipients/{recipientId}/photo`

## Requirements
- Recipient includes name, email, relationship, imageUrl.
- List supports search and paging.
- Photo upload returns imageUrl.
- Recipients are Parties with relationship links, not standalone records.

## Application Services
- RecipientService.CreateAsync
- RecipientService.UpdateAsync
- RecipientService.GetAsync
- RecipientService.ListAsync
- RecipientService.DeleteAsync
- RecipientService.UploadPhotoAsync

## Acceptance Criteria
- Recipient records are tenant scoped.
- Photo upload uses secure storage and signed URLs if needed.
- Deleting recipient does not delete historical orders.
