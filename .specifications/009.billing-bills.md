# 009.billing-bills

## Objective
Expose billing endpoints for bills and outstanding items.

## Scope
- `GET /billing/bills`
- `GET /billing/bills/outstanding`

## Requirements
- Bill list supports status, date range, and paging.
- Outstanding bills return unpaid/overdue bills for customer.
- Bills derive from Billing/Invoicing and are reconciled to ledger state.

## Application Services
- BillingService.ListBillsAsync
- BillingService.ListOutstandingAsync

## Acceptance Criteria
- Responses match MTM bill list structure required by UI.
- Outstanding endpoint only returns open items.
