Rule of use
  • Teal primary on active nav, CTAs, focus rings, eyebrow labels.
  • Teal primary-10 as hover halo behind icons and selected rows.
  • Coral only for pending states, destructive accents, or the secondary CTA pair — never as page chrome.
  • Never combine teal + coral on the same control.
  • The gold mark-dot #E8A838 is reserved to the logo glyph.
