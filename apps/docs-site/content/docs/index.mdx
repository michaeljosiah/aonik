Aonik is the open-core platform for building **AI-native financial products**. You self-host it, plug in your identity provider and third-party integrations, and ship the product your customers need on top.

These docs are for **platform operators and self-hosters** — engineers deploying Aonik, configuring tenants and identity, wiring integrations, and keeping the platform healthy.

<Callout type="info">
  New to Aonik? The [Quickstart](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) gets you a running stack and a bootstrapped tenant in about 15 minutes.
</Callout>

## What's in the box

Aonik ships as a .NET 10 modular monolith fronted by [FastEndpoints](https://fast-endpoints.com), a React 19 admin UI, and a .NET CLI. Out of the box you get:

- **Identity & multi-tenancy** — Auth0 or Microsoft Entra ID, tenant-scoped data on every entity
- **Money movement** — orders, payment intents, double-entry ledger, billing & invoicing
- **Personal finance primitives** — Plaid-linked accounts, transactions, financial life graph, household sharing
- **AI agents** — domain agents with chat and voice surfaces, human-in-the-loop approval for any mutating action
- **Operations** — Quartz-scheduled jobs, audit logs, OpenTelemetry traces

See the [Capability matrix](/docs/getting-started/what-you-get) for the full list of modules.
