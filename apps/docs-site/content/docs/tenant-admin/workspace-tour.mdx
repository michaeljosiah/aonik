## What this page will cover

- The top navigation: tenant switcher, command palette, search, dark/light toggle, profile menu.
- The Dockview workspace: opening panels, drag-to-dock, splitting and grouping, persisting your layout per device.
- The page tree: how pages are categorised; how to pin frequently used pages.
- The notification inbox and how to dismiss / mark read.
- The Agent Playground and its place in QA workflows.
- The Financial Life Graph viewer for inspecting a tenant user's graph.

## In the meantime

Open the Admin UI from your operator-provided URL (`http://localhost:5173` locally) and click around. The UI is intentionally discoverable — every page exposes its actions via the panel header.
