# Understanding compliance rules

Compliance rules ensure onboarding and payments follow regulatory requirements and internal policy.

## Core areas

- KYC and KYB verification workflows.
- Transaction screening and risk alerts.
- Limits by customer tier and corridor.

## Configure responsibly

1. Define risk tiers and required verification steps.
2. Set transaction limits by product and corridor.
3. Document approvals for any exceptions.

## Audit readiness

- Keep policies versioned and change-controlled.
- Track the approvals for high-risk changes.
