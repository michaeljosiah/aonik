# Managing FX rates and spreads

FX configuration controls rate sources, spreads, and refresh schedules.

## Configuration checklist

- Choose your rate providers and update cadence.
- Define spread policies by corridor and customer tier.
- Validate settlement currencies and rounding rules.

## Operational tips

1. Use automated refresh windows during low-latency periods.
2. Monitor rate drift and alert thresholds.
3. Keep pricing policies aligned with ledger postings.
