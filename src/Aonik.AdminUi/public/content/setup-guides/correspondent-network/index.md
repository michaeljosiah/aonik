# Setting up your correspondent network

This guide walks through connecting correspondent partners and establishing routing priorities for cross-border payouts.

## Checklist

- Confirm partner contracts and settlement currencies.
- Capture operational cutoffs and reconciliation windows.
- Define routing priorities by corridor and service level.

## Suggested approach

1. Start with your primary corridor and validate end-to-end flow.
2. Configure fallback partners for redundancy.
3. Verify ledger postings align with the settlement schedule.

## Tips

- Keep routing preferences in one place to simplify audits.
- Validate SLAs and settlement timelines for every partner.
