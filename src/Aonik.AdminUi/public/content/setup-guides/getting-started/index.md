# Getting started with Aonik

Welcome to Aonik. This guide helps you align your team on the launch journey and the platform concepts you will touch during setup.

## What the setup journey covers

- Confirm tenant profile details and operational defaults.
- Define roles, permissions, and who is accountable for each capability.
- Activate catalog services and configure pricing policies.
- Connect routing partners and verify money movement readiness.

## Recommended first actions

1. Assign an owner for each setup step in the journey.
2. Collect configuration inputs (currencies, limits, routing priorities).
3. Walk through the wizard in a staging tenant before production.
